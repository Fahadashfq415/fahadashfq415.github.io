About Me

I am Fahad Ashfaq, a third-semester undergraduate pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Security at the Military College of Signals (MCS), National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST). My academic focus centers on digital forensics, cryptography, network traffic analysis, and secure software development. Through hands-on laboratory work and systems programming in C++ and Java, I enjoy dissecting how data is stored, transmitted, and protected at the low level. Alongside my coursework, I regularly practice disk image analysis, packet inspection, and cryptographic implementation in Linux environments. I am driven by a disciplined, first-principles approach to cybersecurity—building reliable tools and investigating vulnerabilities to better defend modern digital infrastructure.
